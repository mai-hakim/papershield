# PaperShield — demo script

## 75-second story (DEM-05) — one continuous take

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10s | A printed letter in your hand (use the "Possible scam" sample printed on paper, or a real scam letter — DEM-02) | "This came in my mother's mailbox. It says IRS, final notice, pay in 24 hours." |
| 10–20s | Tap the big **Scan a document** button. The camera says "Move closer", then takes the photo itself | "She doesn't need to know anything. One button." |
| 20–30s | The red stamp: **Careful — signs of a scam**. "⚠ 6 scam signs found" | "One answer. Not a summary — a decision." |
| 30–45s | Tap **Show me where it says this** — the gift-card line is highlighted and read aloud | "And it shows why: it asks for gift cards. Real agencies never do." |
| 45–60s | Tap **Send to someone I trust** → preview → WhatsApp | "One tap, and her daughter gets a clear summary — with the account number hidden." |
| 60–75s | Second phone receives the WhatsApp message (DEM-03) | "The letter never left her phone. No account. No upload. It doesn't explain documents. It decides." |

## Two-phone setup (DEM-03)
1. Phone A (parent): open the app → Settings → add Phone B's number as a trusted person, level "Summary and photo".
2. Phone B (child): WhatsApp open.
3. On Phone A, run any sample letter → **Send to someone I trust** → WhatsApp.

## Before / after (DEM-04)
Open `welcome.html` — the second section shows a dense legal paragraph next to one clear result.

## Short how-to video script (DEM-09) — about 45 seconds
1. "Open PaperShield." (Show the home screen.)
2. "Tap the big button and hold the letter flat." (Camera + live hints.)
3. "Check the four corners, then tap Done." (Crop screen.)
4. "Read your answer at the top — the color, the icon and the word." (Result stamp.)
5. "Tap Read it to me to hear it, or I don't understand for simpler words." (Playback + levels.)
6. "Tap Send to someone I trust if you want help." (Share preview.)
7. "Your letter stays on your phone." (Privacy line.)
Record with the phone's screen recorder; add captions in CapCut or Clipchamp.

## Safety notes for the demo
- Use only the built-in sample letters or documents with personal details covered. Never show a real person's account or SSN on stage.
- Say "initial pilot on N documents" — not "proven" (TST-12).
