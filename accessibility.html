PaperShield

Accessibility statement

This page lists what we actually tested. It is not a claim of compliance with Section 508 or WCAG. We built toward those standards; we have not been audited.

Built into the app

  • Atkinson Hyperlegible typeface, designed for readers with low vision.
  • Six presets: standard, larger text, high contrast, hearing support, low vision, color-blind friendly.
  • Every result uses a color, an icon and a word together — never color alone.
  • Large buttons (at least 56 pixels tall) and one-hand mode.
  • Everything can be done by tapping. Nothing requires speaking.
  • Read aloud with pause and stop, a 🐢 to 🐇 speed slider, a mute button for automatic reading, and a Listen button on every screen.
  • Magnifier on the document photo that also reads the line under your finger.
  • Respects “reduce motion” and the phone's dark mode.
  • Screen-reader labels on buttons and a live region for status messages.
