How it works

  1. Scan. Point your phone at the letter. PaperShield tells you to move closer or add light.
  2. Understand. One answer: no action needed, action needed, ask someone you trust, or careful — signs of a scam.
  3. Decide. One sentence tells you what to do, and how many days you have.
  4. See why. It shows the exact line in the letter it based the answer on.
  5. Get help. Send a clear summary to your daughter, son or helper in one tap.
