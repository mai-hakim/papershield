PaperShield

About PaperShield

It doesn't explain documents. It decides.

Why it exists

Bills, insurance notices, government letters and scams all arrive in the same mailbox and look equally official. Many older adults have to guess which ones matter, and scammers count on that.

Who it is for

Older adults who live on their own, and the family members who help them from far away.

What it does

Take a photo of a letter. PaperShield gives one of four answers: no action needed, action needed, ask someone you trust, or careful — signs of a scam. It shows exactly where in the letter it found the reason, and can send a clear summary to a trusted person.

How it stays safe

  • The letter is read on your phone. It is not uploaded.
  • No account, no login, no tracking.
  • Fixed, published rules decide — the same letter always gets the same answer.
  • PaperShield never pays, signs or replies for you.

Our story

PaperShield was designed by Mai Hakim. Mai decided what it does and how it looks, and tested it; the code was written with Claude, an AI assistant. It was inspired by a capstone idea; this is Mai's own independent build.
